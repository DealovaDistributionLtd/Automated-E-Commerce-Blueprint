Core Business Model Canvas

Architectural overview of the automated e-commerce enterprise targeting the Netherlands and Belgium.

1. Value Proposition

  • Fast EU Delivery: 1–3 business days within NL/BE via EU-based supplier warehouses.
  • Localization: Native Dutch customer experience, support, and local product standards.
  • Competitive Pricing: Automated repricing algorithms maintaining margins against buy-box competitors.

2. Revenue & Financial Model

  • Revenue Streams: Retail margins from sales on Amazon.nl and Bol.com.
  • Target Margins: Gross margin 30%–45%; Net margin after fees & returns 12%–18%.
  • Cost Structure: Fixed monthly tools (€50–€200/mo), variable wholesale item prices & 7-15% platform commissions.

3. Compliance Framework

  • Seller of Record (SoR): Registered business name on all invoices, packing slips, and shipping labels.
  • Blind Shipping: Plain, unbranded packaging with zero third-party/retailer marketing.
  • VAT Compliance: Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) registration and EU VAT setup (OSS for cross-border EU sales).
